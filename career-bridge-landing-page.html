Frequently asked questions

Questions worth asking.

Does Career Bridge guarantee employment?

No. We provide career support, recruitment assistance and access to opportunities where available. Hiring decisions belong to employers, and no service can honestly guarantee a job.

Can you help me find remote or international jobs?

We can help source and assess relevant vacancies, including location and work-authorisation requirements. Not every remote vacancy accepts applicants from every country.

Can an employer ask Career Bridge to recruit?

Yes. Employers can contact us with role details, required skills, location, hiring timeline and the type of recruitment support they need.

Do I need to pay before receiving support?

Service scope and fees should be explained and agreed before work begins. We do not charge people for a promise of employment.

Are training programmes available now?

Training dates and programme availability will be published as they are confirmed. Contact us to register interest or discuss a group programme.
